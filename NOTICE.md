# Credits and third-party content

This repository contains Paul Gibbs's work for the November 2026 30DayMapChallenge. An original-code license has not yet been selected.

The challenge name and daily themes come from [30DayMapChallenge](https://30daymapchallenge.com/2026/). Individual projects record their sources and credits in their own READMEs.

## Day 1

- Gorillaz artwork, character illustrations, album imagery, and the tour poster belong to Gorillaz, Jamie Hewlett, and their respective rights holders. The project is an unofficial fan-made atlas.
- [Artwork sources](day1/research/artwork-sources.md) identify official original-image URLs and user-supplied references. Images have been packaged as original assets and arranged through CSS.
- Setlist data is a dated snapshot of community-maintained [setlist.fm](https://www.setlist.fm/setlists/gorillaz-13d681d1.html) concert records. [Source and interpretation notes](day1/research/README.md) accompany the data.
- Country and state-boundary geography comes from Natural Earth and is public domain. Venue-coordinate sources and approximations are documented in the [geography notes](day1/research/geography.md).
- Music plays through Spotify and YouTube's native embeds. No audio recordings are bundled in the repository. [Playback notes](day1/research/music-playback.md) identify mappings, provider behavior, and alternative recordings.
- The application uses Esri's ArcGIS Maps SDK for JavaScript and Calcite Design System through Esri's CDN; those libraries retain their own licenses and attribution.
- Google Fonts serve Barlow Condensed, DM Sans, and Space Mono for the Day 1 interface.

The collection's Day 1 preview image is an application screenshot and includes the same credited imagery. No blanket license to third-party data, artwork, music, fonts, or SDKs is implied by this repository.

## Day 4

- Building footprints are © [OpenStreetMap contributors](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright), distributed under ODbL and accessed through Esri's public OpenStreetMap Buildings for North America service. The app does not bundle a downloaded building database.
- The runtime bundles ArcGIS Maps SDK for JavaScript and Calcite Design System. Their original license notices and third-party notices are preserved in [Day 4 vendor notices](day4/vendor/README.md).
- [Research notes](day4/research-notes.md) attribute the neighborhood concerns to CARB, South Coast AQMD, and CCAEJ. Current footprints do not measure historical growth, exposure, or health outcomes.
