DAY 06 · VINTAGE · NOVEMBER 6, 2026

A new cartridge. A real archipelago.

Travel from Sapporo to Naha in eight levels. Choose Next level to follow the expedition, select any level to jump ahead, or select a beacon on the map. Locate zooms to the current place; Full map restores the overworld. Drag and zoom to explore.

CRT adds a subtle scanline texture. Switch it off for a cleaner view. Your explored locations are remembered on this device when browser storage is available. The final level offers a replay.

What is real?

The coastline and terrain come from geographic data. Terrain is generalized onto a 768 × 768 grid, colored in elevation bands, then enlarged with nearest-neighbor sampling. The eight coordinates are approximate representative points. Beacons, pixel illustrations, the expedition sequence, and the neon palette are original design elements. Dashed connections show the story order; they are not roads, transport routes, or travel advice.
