## Recommended idea

An imagined atlas of the emotional spaces in Travis Scott's UTOPIA. The visitor explores how desire, spectacle, uncertainty, memory, and moments of peace occupy the same world. Central question: **Where does utopia exist for you?**

The strongest organizing idea is a changing relationship to place. The closing dialogue of SIRENS questions the expectation of a perfect destination. In his [PIN–UP design alphabet](https://www.pinupmagazine.org/articles/travis-scott-design-alphabet), Scott describes utopia as something people can create in their own circumstances. These support an atlas with several possible places of fulfillment.

This is our interpretation for a fan-made map, not an official explanation or a claim that the album has one continuous narrative. Track lyrics include guest performances and samples; distinguish their speakers from Scott when writing annotations. The proposed region names, geography, and interactions below are original design choices.

## What the lyrics change about the app

Build one coherent fictional territory with overlapping thematic regions. A track can belong to more than one region. Keep a 19-track index for navigation, but avoid forcing 19 isolated islands or interpreting every title literally.

| Working region | Anchor tracks | Visual language | Interaction |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The Room | SIRENS | Intimate interior opening onto a vast exterior | Enter the atlas from an ordinary place. |
| Spectacle | MODERN JAM, MELTDOWN, FE!N, CIRCUS MAXIMUS, LOOOVE | Concrete plazas, arenas, concentrated light | Follow crowded loops or take quieter exits. |
| Uncertainty | MY EYES, I KNOW ?, SKITZO, LOST FOREVER | Reflections, broken connections, offshore land | Change perspective to uncover a second reading. |
| Echoes | DELRESTO (ECHOES), TIL FURTHER NOTICE | Repeated structures, distant traces, incomplete roads | Reveal connections between memory and departure. |
| Refuge | THANK GOD, GOD'S COUNTRY, PARASAIL | Exposed ridges, shade, moving water | Contrast shelter with the surrounding tension. |
| Possibility | TELEKINESIS | A high, open horizon | Choose an onward path without a declared final answer. |

These are editorial groupings. They are not measured sentiment, audio features, real elevation, or facts about the artist's mental state. GOD'S COUNTRY and TELEKINESIS especially should retain their internal contradictions.

The album sequence is one available route. The thematic layout permits revisits and cross-links. Do not present the record as a simple progression from darkness to happiness: its last track leaves departure unresolved.

## First version to build after concept selection

1. Open on the room from the SIRENS-inspired concept, with an immediate option to enter or skip the introduction.
2. Show the entire atlas, its thematic legend, and a compact track index.
3. Selecting a region or track highlights its geography and opens a brief, original annotation explaining the lyrical connection.
4. Provide a guided excerpt sequence, chapter selection, play/pause, replay, seeking, volume, and a 19-track index for free exploration. Guided order is editorial and must be distinguished from album order.
5. Use actual playback time as the shared clock for audio, map position, layer treatments, annotations, and chapter progress. Pausing or seeking must reconstruct all of them at the same position. Require an explicit start interaction.
6. End with an optional choice of a personal place of utopia. Save locally only if useful; completion should not require an account.

Start with six detailed anchor experiences: SIRENS, MY EYES, CIRCUS MAXIMUS, LOST FOREVER, PARASAIL, and TELEKINESIS, plus a short TIL FURTHER NOTICE epilogue. Target a 4–6 minute guided experience, subject to listening and edit decisions. Include the remaining tracks in the index and regional connections. First prove one MY EYES excerpt with its visual transition, then expand the sequence.

Use charcoal, weathered concrete, warm earth, restrained amber light, and open green/blue areas around refuge. Scott's PIN–UP discussion of Brutalism, landscaping, and virtual spaces gives this material direction a primary-source basis. Color, typography, and particular landforms remain our design decisions. Keep labels readable; offer reduced motion and keyboard equivalents to map selection.

## Pre-build technical options (superseded)

The final app uses dependency-free JavaScript, Canvas 2D, and Web Audio under day8/. Its imagined terrain needs no ArcGIS account, SDK, spatial reference, portal item, or hosted service. The following paragraphs record earlier options rather than the implemented architecture.

The collection already publishes static pages through GitHub Pages. Recent day projects use Vite, TypeScript, ArcGIS Maps SDK for JavaScript 5.1, Map Components, and Calcite 5.1. Those are existing project observations, not a decision to copy dependencies or code from another day.

If ArcGIS is retained, first prove a small 2D schematic map with a fixed extent, authored point/line/polygon graphics, selection, labels, and responsive details. [GraphicsLayer](https://developers.arcgis.com/javascript/latest/references/core/layers/GraphicsLayer/) supports these geometry types. The exact coordinate system and rendering treatment for the fictional terrain need a technical spike; do not claim custom coordinate support has been tested. Do not display Earth coordinates or real distances for invented geography.

Proposed future source folder: day8/scripts/app/. Keep shareable assets and public output under day8/ and use relative build paths. Use local authored map data; there is no concept requirement for geocoding, routing services, hosted editing, private portal content, or a backend. Use the local-audio adapter described in the storyboard. Audio files remain private/local inputs rather than public runtime assets.

Before implementation, settle the fictional/hybrid direction, 2D versus 3D presentation, developer ArcGIS product/account context if ArcGIS is used, and exact resource access. The supplied local recordings are the prototype's reference edition. Final phrase boundaries and musical cue placement need listening review. Original geography and artwork should carry clear credits, and lyric commentary should be labeled as interpretation.

## Acceptance criteria for the eventual prototype

- All 19 track entries match the selected album edition and link to their sources.
- Region selection, track navigation, and details agree; no annotation invents authorial intent.
- Fictional geography and editorial groupings are explained in the legend.
- Core navigation works with keyboard, on a phone, with reduced motion, and without music playback.
- Play, pause, seek, replay, chapter changes, and returning from a background tab preserve a common audio/map/UI position.
- Intentional transitions are the only permitted overlapping audio. Chapter changes cancel stale sources, cues, and camera movements.
- The seven requested delivery MP3s enter the public build. Full source recordings and lossless working copies remain excluded. Missing audio produces an explicit error rather than simulated playback.
- Built output works under the repository's GitHub Pages subpath.
- Run the app's build/type checks and repository checks when implementation exists. No app tests were run during this planning session.
