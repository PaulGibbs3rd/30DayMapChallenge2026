# Seattle in Shadow

**Day 17 · Light & dark · November 17, 2026**

Turn a low black sun across Pioneer Square and the Elliott Bay waterfront. The opening skyline pairs a pale sky with hard monochrome light, inspired by the infrared photography of Giedi Prime in *Dune: Part Two*. [American Society of Cinematographers](https://theasc.com/article/expanding-view-dune-part-two/)

## Explore

- Drag the **Light direction** slider with a pointer or use the arrow keys. **Southwest** and **Northeast** switch between opposite bearings.
- The black disk moves with the light and leaves the frame when its direction falls outside the camera’s view.
- Scrub **Eclipse** to move a second disk across the black sun. **Totality** jumps to full coverage. The view darkens even when the sun is out of frame.
- Drag the scene to pan, right-drag to orbit, or scroll or pinch to zoom. With the scene focused, use arrow keys to pan and A, D, W, and S to rotate and tilt.
- **Reset view** restores the opening camera, northeast light, and clear sun.

## Sources and method

- **Buildings.** The City of Seattle Office of Planning and Community Development published a 3D building model derived from 2015–16 lidar and footprints. The app renders the published shells with matte neutral surfaces and subtle edges. [Seattle 3D Buildings](https://www.arcgis.com/home/item.html?id=fd343811bb604dafa58f23a00e5c0fd0)
- **Streets, shoreline, and terrain.** A custom Esri vector-tile style retains street hierarchy and shoreline shape in charcoal. Esri World Elevation supplies ground elevations. The basemap and historic building model can reflect different dates. [World Basemap](https://basemaps.arcgis.com/arcgis/rest/services/World_Basemap_v2/VectorTileServer) · [World Elevation](https://elevation3d.arcgis.com/arcgis/rest/services/WorldElevation3D/Terrain3D/ImageServer)
- **Shadows.** The slider maps a compass bearing to an illustrative sun position near Pioneer Square. It targets an elevation of 14° and adjusts to roughly 8.5–19.5° over the range of bearings. The same bearing and elevation position the black disk against the sky using the camera’s heading, tilt, and field of view. The disk’s size and halo are stylized. The SDK casts shadows from the building geometry onto the ground and other buildings. [US Naval Observatory solar coordinates](https://aa.usno.navy.mil/faq/sun_approx) · [ArcGIS SunLighting](https://developers.arcgis.com/javascript/latest/references/core/views/3d/environment/SunLighting/)
- **Eclipse.** The coverage readout measures the overlapping area of two equal apparent disks. Coverage drives an art-directed dimming of the rendered city and sky, with a faint corona near totality. ArcGIS continues to render the underlying shadows at the selected bearing and elevation.

## Run locally

The project started with the CDN template generated by `npx -y @arcgis/create --name seattle-in-shadow --template js-maps-sdk-cdn`. It uses the ArcGIS Maps SDK for JavaScript 5.2 Scene component and Calcite Shell. Run `npm run dev` from the repository root and open `/30DayMapChallenge2026/day17/`. The live scene loads public resources over the internet. A local still shows the opening view when the interactive scene is unavailable. [Working spec](spec.md)
