Making a city tile

For Day 21, I used OpenStreetMap to turn a few blocks of Seattle into a 3D-printable city tile. The terrain gives the tile its relief. Building heights, roof shapes, and parts add detail where contributors have mapped them.

Choose a rectangle or hexagon, set the size in millimeters, and preview the STL before importing it into your slicer. I tested a 90 mm terrain tile with a 4 mm flat base, plus a downtown selection with 410 building features in the source area.
